# 几何与代数 1015
## 1. 空间曲线的投影
空间曲线$\Gamma$
$$
\left \{ \begin{aligned}
F(x,y,z) = 0 \\
G(x,y,z) = 0
\end{aligned} \right.
$$
求$\Gamma$在坐标面上的投影曲线
以XY面为例,联立①②消去z即可
得到方程$H(x,y)=0$
它是一个柱面且过曲线$\Gamma$,再将柱面与$z=0$相交得到投影曲线
$$
\left \{ \begin{aligned}
H(x,y) = 0 \\
z = 0 
\end{aligned} \right.
$$

e.g.
$$
\Gamma \left \{ \begin{aligned}
x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = a^2\\
x^2 + y^2 -ax = 0
\end{aligned} \right.
$$
求$\Gamma$在XOY上的投影
$$
\left \{ \begin{aligned}
x^2 + y^2 - ax = 0 \\
z = 0
\end{aligned} \right.
$$
在YOZ面上的投影
$$
\left \{ \begin{aligned}
\frac{z^2}{a^2}(a^2-z^2) = y^2\\
x = 0
\end{aligned} \right.
$$
