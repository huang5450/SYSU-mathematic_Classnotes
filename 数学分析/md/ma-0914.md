- 求导数

$$
f'(x)|_{x=1} = \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(1+\Delta x) - f(1)}{\Delta x} \\
\left( = \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{(1+\Delta x)^2 - 1}{\Delta x} = 2 \right)
$$
分子	
$$
= f(1+\Delta x) - f(1) = (1+\Delta x)^2 - 1 \\
= 2\Delta x + \Delta x^2
$$

$$
\Rightarrow \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(1+\Delta x) - f(1)}{\Delta x} = \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{2\Delta x}{\Delta x} = 2.
$$
